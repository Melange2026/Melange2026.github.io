Our Process

Precision farming, not tradition on autopilot

Every claim we make about this turmeric traces back to a specific practice, on our farm — because we'd rather get it right than just say it.

01

The Farm

No soil means no soil-borne pathogens. No open fields means no airborne residue. No guessing means no compromise. That's our farm.

02

The Tech

Irrigation, fertigation and climate are sensor-logged in real time and run on solar power with battery backup. Every input is metered, not guessed.

03

Scientifically Grown

Root-zone analysis every 30 days tracks curcumin build-up. Protection is chemical-free biopesticides. At harvest: a lab-verified assay, available on request.

04

Traceable

Every batch traces back to Melange Farms, one harvest season, one verified source. No pooling. No blending. Complete accountability.
