The Story

Precision farming, not tradition on autopilot

Every claim we make about this turmeric traces back to a specific practice, on a specific farm — not an industry average.

01 · The Farm

A controlled structure, a clean media

Turmeric grown inside a 1.25-acre shadenet — 9,800 grow bags across 12 zones — using the Pratibha variety in pure, washed, low-EC cocopeat instead of open soil. Not open-field growing left to chance.

02 · The Tech

Solar-powered, sensor-monitored, built to last

Irrigation, fertigation, and climate are sensor-logged in real time and run on solar power with battery backup — pumping is even timed to peak solar generation. Every input is metered, not guessed.

03 · Scientifically Grown

Verified nutrients, biological protection

Root-zone analysis every 30 days tracks curcumin build-up months before harvest. The shadenet keeps outside pests out; what's left is handled with chemical-free biopesticides. At harvest: a lab-verified assay, free of harmful metal and pesticide residues, available on request.

04 · Traceable

One farm. One grower. No blending.

This turmeric isn't pooled from unnamed sources. It's one sustainable, water- and soil-preserving operation, one season, one person accountable end to end: Melange Farms, Karnataka.
